Birds in the Air Baby Quilt

Birds in the Air baby quilt

A 3 by 4 block layout, about 36 by 48 inches with 12 inch blocks. White background, scrap fabrics for the large triangle and one small triangle in each block. Tap a colored triangle, then choose a fabric color.

Tap a colored triangle, then pick a color.

Each block uses one large colored triangle, one small colored triangle that pairs with a white one to make the top right half-square triangle, and two more white small triangles. Sizes follow the QuiltGrids Birds in the Air pattern. The 6 in squares in the fabric stack suit the smaller block, which would need about 6 by 8 blocks for a baby quilt.
